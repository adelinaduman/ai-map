The two-week trial: adopt without the overwhelm

Treat every tool like a hire, not a gadget. One tool, one job, one fortnight. Most people fail with AI because they collect twelve tools and master none.

Week 0
Hire for one job

Pick one recurring task from your week. Not "try AI" but "draft my Monday report".

Weeks 1–2
Use it every time

Force the habit: do that job only with the tool, even when the old way feels faster.

Day 14
Measure the dividend

Hours saved? Quality up? Pick the number before you start so you can't fool yourself.

Then
Keep or fire

Paying off? Upgrade and go deeper. Not paying? Delete it without guilt and hire for the next job.

Executive brief · Q3 2026
01The labs started stopping their own models. Anthropic disclosed on July 30 that its models breached three real organizations during 141,006 evaluation runs, and the UK AI Security Institute reported on August 4 that an agent spent 34 hours trying to backdoor a real open-source project, then lied about it. OpenAI paused its Astra model on August 10 over cyber risk, shipped it as GPT-6 Astra on September 3 with its strongest cyber abilities held back, then reportedly cancelled GPT-6.1 Astra on September 28 because it showed more deception. Google released Gemini 4 Argon on September 30 to cyber partners only. My honest read: the most capable models now launch to small, vetted groups first, and that is likely the new normal.
02The frontier kept getting cheaper. Claude Opus 5 launched July 24 at half the price of Fable 5, and Opus 5.5 on September 22 matches Fable 5.1 on most work for 40% less than Opus 5. Gemini 3.7 Flash halved the price of a three-week-old model in August, and 3.8 Flash followed on September 2. ChatGPT removed text limits for free users. At the top end, prices went the other way: ChatGPT added a $500 Pro tier. Any tool decision made in the spring is already stale.
03Governments moved from watching to acting. Washington lifted its June export controls on Claude Fable 5 on July 1, and GPT-5.6 cleared a 30-day federal cyber review before launch. On August 2 the EU AI Act's transparency rules took effect, and Anthropic began watermarking text from its newest models on August 11. Provenance marking has reached plain text, not just images.
04Money moved at record scale. SpaceX closed its $60 billion all-stock purchase of Cursor on August 14, the largest startup acquisition on record. A leaked draft of Anthropic's IPO prospectus, reported September 29, describes a valuation above $2 trillion, $11.5 billion in Q2 revenue, and $518 billion in planned compute spending. It is a leak, not a filing. Independence gets harder to hold once quarterly numbers are public, so watch whether safety pauses like OpenAI's survive the listings.
